# Cosmos3 Certified NIM acknowledgements

The running NIM exposes bundled product license information at `/v1/license`.
The release image also includes:

- `/opt/nim/LICENSE` for the NIM terms;
- `/opt/nim/MODEL_LICENSE` for the model terms;
- `/opt/nim/NOTICE` for product notices and contact information;
- `/opt/nim/licenses/` for included component licenses and notices; and
- `/usr/share/cosmos3/oss-source/` for the exact package-modification script,
  machine-readable source manifest, reproduction instructions, and checksums
  associated with removal of unused Berkeley DB adapters.

The cookbook source is covered by the repository
[LICENSE](../../../LICENSE). Inspect the files in the exact image you deploy so
the terms and notices match that image.
